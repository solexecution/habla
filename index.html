Recommended session

Spaced repetition mixes new words with the ones you're about to forget — the fastest proven way to make them stick.

How it works

1. Flip & rate. See the Spanish, guess, flip, then say how it went — Again / Hard / Good.

2. Come back tomorrow. Words you rate Good return less often; Again brings them right back.

3. Everything saves on your phone. Progress lives in this app and works with no signal. Add it to your home screen from your browser menu (⋮ → Install app).

Why this works

Spacing + retrieval. You review each word just before you'd forget it, and you recall it rather than reread it — the two most robust findings in memory research.

Produce, don't just recognise. In Recall you type the Spanish from memory (or go hands-free in the car). Generating an answer builds far stronger memory than picking from options.

Words in context. Every card carries an example sentence and native audio, so you learn the word the way you'll actually use it.
